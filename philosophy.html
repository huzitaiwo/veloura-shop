The Veloura house

Made for the spaces between.

Veloura began with a wish for beauty care that feels informed and sensorial, but never demanding.

A quieter approach

“Care should offer a way back to yourself.”

We look at skincare through the lens of use: the way a texture lands on the skin, the small satisfaction of a clear routine, and the lasting pleasure of choosing less, but better.

Our evolving collection is a study in these moments. Each formula is introduced with context, ingredient transparency, and enough space for you to decide what belongs on your shelf.
